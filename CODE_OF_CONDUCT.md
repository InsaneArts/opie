# Code of conduct

This repository is for talking about Opie. Keep it worth reading.

**Be plain and be kind.** Disagree with the argument, not the person. Assume the other person is trying to help and has less context than you do, not that they are stupid.

**Stay on the topic.** Issues are for bugs and requests, discussions are for everything else. Politics, promotion, and recruiting belong elsewhere.

**Do not post what is not yours to post.** No credentials, no other people's files, filenames, or screenshots with their data in them. Read a log before you paste it.

**Harassment ends participation.** Insults, slurs, sexual attention, threats, following someone across threads, or publishing someone's private information: any of these gets you removed, on the first occurrence if it is bad enough.

## Enforcement

Comments that break these rules get edited or deleted, and accounts that keep breaking them are blocked from the repository. Report anything you see to the maintainers through a [private vulnerability report](https://github.com/InsaneArts/opie/security/advisories/new) if it is sensitive, or by mentioning a maintainer if it is not. Reports are handled privately, and what you report is not shared with the person you report.

The decisions here are made by the maintainers of Opie, and they are final.
